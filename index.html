RT Handbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

RT Handbook is provided by Black Lab NDT inc. ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how the Android app handles information when you use its radiography tools, inspection notes, voice features, and export functions.

Privacy contact: blacklabndt@gmail.com

At a glance

  • Your notes and settings are stored on your device. You do not need an RT Handbook account.
  • Voice processing defaults to Phone only, which transcribes speech on your device.
  • If you choose Auto or Cloud only and supply an OpenAI API key, the app sends speech and related information directly to OpenAI for processing.
  • The app does not include advertising, advertising trackers, or automatic analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • You control exports, sharing, printing, and backup locations.

Information the app handles

Inspection records and settings. The app stores information you enter, import, or generate, including jobs, client labels, daily lists, dates, inspection notes, connection and weld identifiers, welder IDs, pipe and fitting details, quantities, and inspection numbering. It also stores calculator inputs, saved setups, calibration values, preferences, and locally learned note-entry corrections. These records may contain personal or confidential business information depending on what you enter.

Voice information. When you start voice input, the app captures audio from your selected phone or Bluetooth microphone. It processes audio to create transcripts and inspection entries. Audio samples are held temporarily in memory during processing; the app does not maintain an audio-recording archive. Transcripts, draft entries, review suggestions, and recovery information may be saved locally. Recovery information can include the microphone device name, processing mode, and processing errors.

Optional cloud settings. If you configure cloud voice processing, the app stores your processing preference, API key, spending-guard setting, and a local estimate of API usage charges. The API key is encrypted locally using a key protected by Android Keystore. The app does not collect payment-card information; OpenAI handles any API billing through your provider account.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Avoid sending API keys or unnecessary personal or confidential inspection data.

How information is used

The app uses information to perform calculations, organize inspection records, recognize and interpret dictated notes, restore unfinished work, remember preferences, operate timers, and create the exports or backups you request. We do not sell personal information or use app content for targeted advertising.

Normal app operation does not upload your notes to a server operated by Black Lab NDT inc. We do not have remote access to records stored only on your device.

Voice processing and OpenAI

Phone only is the default. In this mode, bundled speech-recognition software processes your audio locally. The app does not send your audio or transcripts to OpenAI in this mode.

Auto and Cloud only are optional. When you select one of these modes and provide an API key, the app sends audio segments and relevant welder IDs from the active list to OpenAI for transcription. It can also send the session transcript for interpretation into structured inspection instructions. Anything spoken in those segments, including names, identifiers, or confidential details, may therefore be included. Requests use your API key and are sent directly from your device over HTTPS. OpenAI also receives connection information, such as your IP address, needed to provide its service.

Auto attempts cloud processing first and falls back to phone processing if the cloud attempt fails. A failed request may already have transmitted information. Cloud only stops processing on failure rather than switching to local transcription.

OpenAI processes API content under the terms and data controls applicable to your OpenAI account. Provider retention depends on the endpoint and applicable account settings; choosing cloud processing does not mean information is immediately deleted by the provider. The app requests that cloud interpretation results not be stored as saved API completions, but this does not eliminate every form of provider retention. See OpenAI's API data controls for details. OpenAI's handling of your provider account is described in its applicable privacy policy.

Cloud processing may involve processing outside your province or country. Use Phone only if you do not want voice content sent to OpenAI. You can change the mode and remove the saved API key in Voice processing settings when recording is stopped. These changes prevent future cloud use through that configuration; they do not recall information already transmitted.

Sharing, exports, backups, and external links

The app shares information when you choose an export, share, print, or backup action. Your selected receiving app, printer service, file provider, or storage provider receives the content needed to complete that action and handles it under its own practices. Choosing a cloud storage location may upload the file to that provider.

CSV exports contain the selected inspection data. Notes backup files contain the notes library and may include drafts, transcripts, and recovery or undo information retained within it. Export and backup files are not encrypted by RT Handbook. Store and share them only with intended recipients.

The Android app is configured to disable standard Android app backup. Manual notes backups remain available. Device manufacturers and device-transfer tools may have their own behavior.

If you open an external reference link, your browser connects to the third-party website, which may receive connection information and use its own cookies or tracking technologies. This policy covers RT Handbook, not those websites or other apps.

Device permissions

  • Microphone: captures speech for voice input you start. A recording session can continue while the app is in the background; use Stop to end it or revoke microphone permission in Android settings.
  • Bluetooth connection: supports recording through a connected headset when selected.
  • Notifications and foreground services: show and maintain active recording or timer functions.
  • Display over other apps: supports floating timer controls when enabled.
  • Alarms, vibration, audio controls, and wake locks: support countdown alerts, microphone routing, and active processing.
  • Internet: enables optional cloud voice processing.

The app does not request access to your contacts, precise device location, camera, or photo library. Files used for backup and restore are selected through Android's file picker. You can manage permissions through Android settings; refusing a permission may limit the related feature.

Retention and deletion

On-device records. Notes, settings, and saved work remain until you delete or replace them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Some deleted or edited content can remain in undo history, drafts, or recovery records. The native voice recovery journal can retain the latest session until it is replaced by a later session. Deleting a visible entry does not necessarily remove all recovery copies.

Shared-file cache. The app creates temporary CSV files for sharing. It attempts to remove cached shares older than 24 hours when you next create a share; this is not a guaranteed 24-hour deletion deadline.

Removing local app data. To remove all locally stored app records, settings, and recovery data, stop recording and use Android Settings → Apps → RT Handbook → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. Clearing only the cache does not delete all saved records. Removing the saved API key in the app does not revoke the key at OpenAI; you can revoke it through your provider account.

Copies outside the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete exports, backups, printed documents, files held by recipients, or information previously sent to OpenAI. Delete external copies through the relevant file location or service. Restoring a backup can restore information previously deleted from the app.

Support correspondence. We retain support and privacy correspondence for as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you have sent us.

Security

The app uses Android's app storage protections, HTTPS for cloud API requests, and Android Keystore-backed encryption for the saved API key. It does not add separate encryption to all inspection records or export files. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep it updated, and protect any backups you create. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can use manual note entry, keep voice processing on Phone only, stop recording, revoke device permissions, remove your saved API key, edit or delete records, and choose whether and where to export information.

Depending on the laws that apply, you may have rights to access or correct personal information, request deletion, withdraw consent, or raise a privacy concern. Contact blacklabndt@gmail.com to make a request about information held by Black Lab NDT inc. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We cannot retrieve or remotely erase information stored only on your device, or erase copies controlled by another recipient or provider. We can explain the available local controls and where to direct a provider request.

Children

RT Handbook is intended for professional inspection work and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

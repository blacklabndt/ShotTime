About this chart

Published carbon-steel dimensions from Weldbend catalogue pages 138–143 and Alaskan Copper catalogue page 43. Only listed sizes and schedules are shown. Stainless S schedules are not included.

Metric dimensions are converted from inches. Inside diameter is calculated as outside diameter minus twice the wall thickness; actual dimensions vary with manufacturing tolerances.

XH = XS (extra heavy / extra strong). XXH = XXS. STD and XH are separate designations and do not always equal schedules 40 and 80.

Film calibration factors

Film-wide factors multiply size/schedule factors. Enter a factor for each film, based on a verified technique shot at your target density. Factor = verified exposure time ÷ unadjusted app time, using the same activity and geometry. 1.00 leaves estimates unchanged; 1.10 adds 10%. Factors apply to all sizes for that film and need validation across your working range.

Size / schedule correction

Applies only to the selected pipe, schedule, film and density. Final time = baseline time × film-wide factor × size/schedule factor. Internal times use that corrected time too.

CS Flex HR calibration

CS Flex HR uses no chart. Time = plate exposure (R) × SFD² ÷ Ir-192 output (0.48 R·m² per Ci·h) × the steel curve ÷ activity. Each pipe card has a PMT slider in steps of 10 and shows the plate exposure your reference shots imply at that setting. Shots read at another PMT are converted by your gain measurements, or else by the built-in law from your PMT 20 and 40 shots (gain doubling every 12.4 PMT). With no usable shots a card falls back to the plate exposure your Carestream chart implies for its pipe size, from 5 R at 2" down to 0.65 R at 24". The steel curve defaults to 14.5 mm, also from that chart (published values 12–13.6 mm are for film). Reference shots below show the plate exposure they delivered, and at their PMT they set the suggested value; a shot at another PMT is converted only with your measured scanner gain. Good brightness is not image quality: check the IQI.

      Chart setup & calculation details

      Shot time = reference Ci·s × (SFD ÷ reference SFD)² × 2^(steel difference ÷ 12.1 mm) × film factor × size/schedule factor ÷ source activity. Steel is the total of both walls. Film factors apply across that film’s pipe sizes. Size/schedule factors apply to one combination. Both are saved separately for each target density; 1.00 means no additional correction.

      Reference shots: Large films use approximate calculator targets on 4-inch STD at 26 Ci. At densities 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5 and 4.0: D5 11, 15, 19, 23 and 27 seconds; T200 16.8, 21.6, 26.4, 30.6 and 34.8 seconds; Fuji 80 13, 18, 23, 28 and 33 seconds. Density 3.5 is interpolated. Small films are tied to their large-film partner at the same geometry and density using published Ir-192 relative exposures: D4 = D5 × 2.0, MX125 = T200 × 1.65, Fuji 50 = Fuji 80 × 1.87, so partners aim at the same density at every target. On 3-inch STD at 26 Ci and density 3.0 that gives D4 22, MX125 25 and Fuji 50 24 seconds. These are not measured-density calibrations for your processing.

      CS Flex HR: Uses the calibrated plate-exposure model and your reference shots, scanner PMT and pixel target. See CS Flex HR calibration for its settings and calculation details.

      Internal times, 6 inches and larger: Internal = unrounded shot time ÷ 9, rounded to the nearest second. Internal offset = unrounded shot time ÷ 3.2, rounded to the nearest second. CS Flex HR uses these internal-time conversions: Internal ÷ 8, Internal offset ÷ 3, rounded up. These user-defined conversions do not independently model internal geometry.

      Setup: Carbon steel, double wall, lead screens. 1-inch pipe uses 12-inch SFD; other sizes use OD +0.125 inches. Processing reference: manual 4 minutes at 72°F with Carestream INDUSTREX Single Part developer.

      Calculation basis: Exposure follows the inverse-square law for SFD and doubles every 12.1 mm of steel, the Ir-192 half-value thickness measured from the Agfa/GE Structurix exposure diagram. The same steel curve applies to every film. Verify the reference shots with test films and correct with film factors. No source-transit, weld reinforcement, oblique-path or geometric-unsharpness correction. Validate corrections across the sizes you use.

      Dark theme: Reduce phone brightness before entering the darkroom. This screen is not a film-safe safelight. Keyboard, notifications and print dialogs are controlled by Android and may be bright.

      Privacy: Works offline. No accounts or tracking. Film choices and last calculated source activity are saved on this phone. Source reference, filters, film factors and display preferences are also saved locally.

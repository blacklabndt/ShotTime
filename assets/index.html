Film calibration factors

Film-wide factors multiply size/schedule factors. Enter a factor for each film, based on a verified technique shot at your target density. Factor = verified exposure time ÷ unadjusted app time, using the same activity and geometry. 1.00 leaves estimates unchanged; 1.10 adds 10%. Factors apply to all sizes for that film and need validation across your working range.

Chart setup & calculation details

Shot time = reference Ci·s × (SFD ÷ reference SFD)² × 2^(steel difference ÷ 12.1 mm) × film factor × size/schedule factor ÷ source activity. Steel is the total of both walls. Film factors apply across that film’s pipe sizes. Size/schedule factors apply to one combination. Both are saved separately for each target density; 1.00 means no additional correction.

Reference shots: Large films use approximate calculator targets on 4-inch STD at 26 Ci. At densities 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5 and 4.0: D5 11, 15, 19, 23 and 27 seconds; T200 14, 18, 22, 25.5 and 29 seconds; Fuji 80 13, 18, 23, 28 and 33 seconds. Density 3.5 is interpolated. Small films are tied to their large-film partner at the same geometry and density using published Ir-192 relative exposures: D4 = D5 × 2.0, MX125 = T200 × 1.65, Fuji 50 = Fuji 80 × 1.83, so partners aim at the same density at every target. On 3-inch STD at 26 Ci and density 3.0 that gives D4 22, MX125 21 and Fuji 50 24 seconds. These are not measured-density calibrations for your processing.

Internal times, 6 inches and larger: Internal = unrounded shot time ÷ 9, rounded to the nearest second. Internal offset = unrounded shot time ×1.15 ÷4, rounded up. These user-defined conversions do not independently model internal geometry.

Setup: Carbon steel, double wall, lead screens. 1-inch pipe uses 12-inch SFD; other sizes use OD +0.125 inches. Processing reference: manual 4 minutes at 72°F with Carestream INDUSTREX Single Part developer.

Calculation basis: Exposure follows the inverse-square law for SFD and doubles every 12.1 mm of steel, the Ir-192 half-value thickness measured from the Agfa/GE Structurix exposure diagram. The same steel curve applies to every film. Verify the reference shots with test films and correct with film factors. No source-transit, weld reinforcement, oblique-path or geometric-unsharpness correction. Validate corrections across the sizes you use.

Dark theme: Reduce phone brightness before entering the darkroom. This screen is not a film-safe safelight. Keyboard, notifications and print dialogs are controlled by Android and may be bright.

Privacy: Works offline. No accounts, tracking or network permission. Film choices and last calculated source activity are saved on this phone. Source reference, filters, film factors and display preferences are also saved locally. Decay mode updates daily from the saved reference; manual mode uses the entered activity.

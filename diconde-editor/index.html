Display (does not change pixels)

EDGE-style enhancement (display)

An approximation of INDUSTREX's EDGE display filter: large-scale (thickness) contrast is reduced and fine detail is raised. Carestream has not published its algorithm, so the look will differ from INDUSTREX. Setting 1.0 is this editor's default strength, tuned against an INDUSTREX EDGE HD 1 screenshot; it is close but not identical. The enhancement is shown on screen and in PNG/PDF exports; saved files are unchanged unless you choose Apply to pixels.

Undo

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, F fit, V rotate view, I info overlay, hold C to compare with the original, Esc clears the crop box, right-drag adjusts the window.

Processed copies are marked DERIVED and record each step in Derivation Description. Check your procedure or code before using enhanced images for acceptance.

Crop

Choose the Crop tool and drag on the image, or type the box in image pixels.

Split into separate files

Finds each piece of film or plate by the background around it, including slightly tilted dividing lines between pieces scanned together. Check the regions before saving; you can adjust, remove or redraw them.

Or choose the Regions tool and drag boxes on the image.

Each part is saved as its own DICONDE file with your metadata, a new instance UID, a shared new series, and a reference to the original.
